ASADBEK AI v8 — REAL MAPS
==========================
Real-world map support was added using OpenStreetMap.

City anchors:
1. Samarqand — Registon: 39.6542, 66.9597
2. Andijon shahri: 40.7821, 72.3442
3. Toshkent: 41.3111, 69.2797

The REAL XARITA button opens an OpenStreetMap view centered on the selected city.
This is a real geographic map. The 3D game world remains a lightweight optimized
scene; it is NOT a 1:1 photogrammetry reconstruction of every building.

Internet is required for live OSM tiles. OpenStreetMap attribution is displayed.
